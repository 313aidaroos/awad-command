# Lyrixis publisher desk (50 / one-send)

Not a harvester. You supply the 50 rows.

Allowed sources
- People who wrote lyrixis@apixis.dev or the waitlist
- Public partnership / rights emails printed on a publisher site (press@, rights@, audio@)
- Your own conference list

Not allowed
- Scraping personal Gmail
- Bought lists
- A second email if they did not reply

Flow
1. Drop CSV: name, house, role, email, why_they_fit
2. Cixy drafts 50 letters from `src/projects/lyrixis/outreachTemplate.ts`
3. Each draft lands in Mailroom as waiting_approval
4. You Approve → one send. Stop.

Subject line and body stay short. No fake Amazon claim.
